Privacy Policy – Islamic Habit Companion

Effective Date: February 2026

Islamic Habit Companion is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

a) Location Information

The app may access your device’s approximate location (via GPS or network-based location) to provide accurate prayer times. Your location data is used only to calculate prayer timings and is not stored permanently on external servers.

b) Usage Data

The app stores worship tracking data such as Salah completion, Dhikr counts, and goals locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to any external server.

c) Advertising Data

The app may use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, and usage data to provide personalized or non-personalized ads. This data is handled according to Google's Privacy Policy.

d) In-App Purchases

If you purchase a premium feature (such as ad removal), the transaction is processed securely through Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your payment information.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used solely to:

  • Provide accurate prayer times based on location
  • Maintain your daily worship tracking data locally
  • Display advertisements (in the free version)
  • Enable premium features through in-app purchases

3. Data Storage

All worship tracking data (Salah, Dhikr, Goals, Charity tracking) is stored locally on your device. We do not operate or maintain a remote server for storing your personal religious data.

4. Third-Party Services

The app may use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob (for advertisements)
  • Google Play Billing (for in-app purchases)
  • Aladhan API (for prayer time calculations)

These services may collect information as governed by their own privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Islamic Habit Companion does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties. Advertising partners may collect anonymized device data as described above.

7. Your Control

You may:

  • Disable location permission from device settings
  • Remove app data from device storage
  • Purchase premium to remove ads

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.
